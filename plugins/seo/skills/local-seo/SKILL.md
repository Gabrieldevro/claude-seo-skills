# Local SEO

Business: `$ARGUMENTS`.

## 1. Facts to collect (ask only for what you can't find on the site)
Exact business name, address (or service area), phone, website, hours, categories, services with prices, cities served, years in business, licences/certifications, review count/rating, competitors that rank in the map pack.

## 2. Deliverables

### Google Business Profile
- Primary category (the most specific one that matches what people search) + up to 9 secondary categories.
- Business description (≤750 chars, first 250 matter most): services, area, differentiator — no URLs, no promo language.
- Services list with short descriptions and prices; products if relevant.
- Attributes, hours (including holiday hours), booking/appointment URL with UTM (`?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`).
- Photo plan: exterior, interior, team, work before/after, at least weekly.
- Posts calendar: 1/week (offer, project, tip).
- Q&A: 5 questions customers ask, answered by the owner.

### NAP consistency
One canonical form of Name, Address, Phone. List where it must match exactly: website footer, contact page, schema, GBP, Facebook, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, and local directories relevant to the country.

### Website
- One landing page per **service × city** that has real demand — unique content each (local photos, projects done there, prices, travel area, FAQs), not city-name swaps.
- Title pattern: `Service in City – Benefit | Brand`; H1 with service + city; embedded map on the contact page; click-to-call.
- `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD (most specific subtype) with `geo`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `areaServed`, `sameAs` → GBP and social profiles.

### Reviews
A simple process: ask after a successful job, short link to the GBP review form, reply to every review within 48 h (template replies for 5★, 3★, 1★ in the business's language). Never offer incentives for reviews.
